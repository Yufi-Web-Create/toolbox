# Product Definition v0.1

## Product type
Multi-tenant SaaS web application.

## Goal
Provide one workspace for inbound communication, outbound social publishing, AI-assisted replies, templates, and calendar-related workflow.

## Core modules

### Inbox
- Email
- Instagram DM
- Facebook Messenger
- Status: unread / in progress / completed
- Assignee
- Conversation history

### Posts
- Instagram
- Threads
- Facebook
- X
- Multi-platform publishing
- Scheduled publishing
- Platform-specific AI rewriting

### Templates & AI
- Saved reply templates
- Variable insertion
- Knowledge base
- AI reply drafts
- Translation
- Conversation summaries

### Calendar
- Google Calendar connection
- Read/create/update events
- Availability checks
- Detect dates/times from inquiries
- Associate conversations with calendar events

## Product principle
AI assists humans. Initial versions must not automatically send AI-generated replies without human confirmation.
